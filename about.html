Our Story

The House of Zare

Chapter One

Created with a purpose, rooted in modesty.

Label by Zare began with a simple belief: modesty and elegance can beautifully coexist. What started as a passion for creating graceful, modest pieces grew into a dream of building something of our own — a space where women can find abayas that feel beautiful, comfortable and true to their values.

Every piece at Label by Zare is chosen and created with intention. From the fabric and silhouette to the smallest details, we believe that modest clothing doesn’t have to mean compromising on style.

Our journey is still growing, one piece and one customer at a time. And we’re grateful to every woman who chooses to be a part of it.

Thank you for being here. 🤍
— Label by Zare

Chapter Two

How we build a piece

Every silhouette goes through several rounds of fitting on real bodies, not just a stand form. We check how a piece moves when you sit, reach, and walk — not just how it hangs still.

Construction details — hidden closures, reinforced seams, a tailored shoulder — are chosen for how they hold up over months of wear, not just how they look on day one.
