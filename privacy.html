Privacy Policy

Effective: 8 September 2026 · Last updated: 8 September 2026

This policy explains what information Label by Zare collects when you browse our store, create an account, place an order, contact us, join our newsletter or choose to allow optional cookies.

Information we collect

For orders and customer support we may collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, city, area, postal code, order contents and order history. If you create an account, authentication and account information is processed through our authentication provider. Contact messages, newsletter subscriptions, saved addresses, wishlist and cart information may also be stored when you use those features.

Essential aggregate traffic counting

We count storefront page loads in aggregate even when optional cookies are rejected. This essential counter stores only the date, page path and a total count. It does not create a visitor identifier or session identifier and does not store a raw IP address, device fingerprint, active time or cross-page behavioral profile.

Optional cookies and detailed analytics

Detailed browsing analytics is optional. Until you choose “Accept all” or otherwise allow optional cookies, we do not create our persistent analytics visitor identifier or send detailed first-party browsing analytics. If you allow optional cookies, we may record page views, product interactions, approximate country/city/region, device/browser type, referral or campaign information, and active time. Purchase events used in our first-party reports are created only after an order is accepted. We do not intentionally store raw IP addresses in our analytics tables, and we do not use analytics to determine a street address.

Services that process information

We use Cloudflare to deliver and protect the website and to provide coarse request geography, Supabase for database and authentication services, Formspree for order notification delivery, and Google Analytics only if a valid analytics configuration is enabled and you have accepted optional cookies. Each provider may process technical information required to operate its service under its own terms.

Why we use information

  • To process, deliver, track and support orders.
  • To provide accounts, saved carts, wishlists and saved addresses.
  • To answer support requests and handle returns or exchanges.
  • To prevent abuse, diagnose reliability problems and secure the store.
  • To count aggregate storefront traffic without identifying individual visitors.
  • With your choice, to understand detailed store usage and improve products, pages and marketing.

Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for orders, customer service, accounting, fraud prevention, legal obligations and legitimate business records. We use access controls, row-level database security and encrypted HTTPS connections, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can shop with essential site functions while rejecting non-essential cookies. Aggregate page-load counts may still be recorded without identifiers. Optional detailed analytics remains disabled unless you accept optional cookies. You may also contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of eligible personal information. Some order records may need to be retained where required for business or legal reasons.
