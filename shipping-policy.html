Shipping Policy

Effective: 8 September 2026 · Last updated: 8 September 2026

Label by Zare currently delivers within Pakistan. Shipping charges and the final delivery method are validated when an order is submitted.

Processing

Orders are normally prepared within 1–2 business days before courier transit. High-volume periods, product checks or address verification may extend processing.

Standard delivery

Our current standard delivery guidance is approximately 3–5 business days nationwide after processing. The current standard fee configured at checkout is PKR 350, with free standard shipping when the order reaches the currently configured free-shipping threshold.

Express delivery

Where offered, express delivery is intended for approximately 1–2 business days in major cities after processing. Availability and the live fee are confirmed by checkout; some destinations may not receive the same transit time.

Addresses and delays

Please provide a complete address and reachable phone number. Courier, weather, public holiday, remote-area or recipient-availability delays may occur after dispatch. Use Track Order or contact us if a shipment needs attention.

Questions

For a delivery question, use Track Order or contact us.
