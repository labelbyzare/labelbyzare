Returns & Exchanges Policy

Effective: 8 September 2026 · Last updated: 8 September 2026

We want you to be comfortable with your purchase. The current return window is 7 days from delivery for eligible pieces.

Eligibility

Unless a product or promotion states otherwise, eligible items should be unworn, unused, unwashed, undamaged and returned with original tags attached. We may ask for order information and photos where condition or damage needs to be assessed.

Damaged or incorrect items

If an item arrives damaged or is not the item you ordered, start a claim promptly through the order-support flow and provide the requested details. Responsibility for return delivery may differ for verified damaged/incorrect items.

Change-of-mind refunds

For a non-damaged refund request, current store policy assigns return-delivery responsibility to the customer. Original delivery charges may not be refundable where permitted and where they were already incurred.

Exchanges

Exchange availability depends on current stock and the requested replacement option. An exchange is not reserved until the request is accepted.

How to start

Use the Refund / Exchange section with your order number and email, or contact us through the official support channels shown on the site.

Refund timing

Where a refund is approved, processing timing can depend on the original payment method and operational checks. Cash on Delivery refunds may require us to obtain appropriate refund details through a secure support interaction.
