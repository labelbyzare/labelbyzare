02 — Delivery

Shipping & Returns

I.

Standard Delivery

3–5 business days nationwide. PKR 350, free on orders over PKR 15,000.

II.

Express Delivery

1–2 business days in major cities. PKR 900, or free on orders over PKR 15,000.

III.

Returns & Exchanges

Unworn pieces with tags attached may be returned within 7 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days of being placed. To start a return or exchange, message us on WhatsApp with your order number.
